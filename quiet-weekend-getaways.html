Quiet Weekend Getaways

Ideas for leaving the usual routine behind and finding a little breathing room in a beautiful place.

Travel 7 min read Evermere Journal

A weekend away does not have to involve a packed itinerary or a long list of places to visit. Sometimes the best escape is simply finding somewhere peaceful enough to let the usual rhythm slow down.

Quiet getaways can be surprisingly simple. A small town, a coastal stay, a countryside guesthouse, or a comfortable room overlooking a different landscape can be enough to create a sense of distance from everyday routines.

The idea is not necessarily to see everything. Instead, it is about creating a little space between one weekend and the next, with enough time to sleep well, wander slowly, and enjoy your surroundings.

Choose a slower destination

When planning a peaceful weekend, the destination matters less than the atmosphere. Look for places where the surroundings naturally encourage a slower pace.

A walkable town centre, a quiet coastline, open countryside, or a property surrounded by trees can all create that feeling of stepping away without requiring an elaborate plan.

Places to consider
  • Small towns with cafés and independent shops.
  • Coastal areas where mornings begin slowly.
  • Countryside stays surrounded by open scenery.
  • Peaceful neighbourhoods close to nature.

Give yourself less to do

One of the easiest ways to make a weekend feel restorative is to resist the temptation to fill every hour. Leave room for an unplanned breakfast, a long walk, or simply sitting somewhere pleasant with no particular destination in mind.

Instead of building a detailed schedule, choose one or two things you genuinely want to experience and let everything else remain optional.

That extra breathing room can often become the most memorable part of the trip.

Make the journey part of it

The feeling of a getaway can begin before you arrive. If possible, choose a route that gives you time to enjoy the scenery instead of treating the journey as something to rush through.

A scenic drive, an easy train journey, or even a relaxed stop for coffee can make the transition from everyday life to weekend mode feel more natural.

The best weekend plans often leave enough empty space for something unexpected to become part of the experience.

Find a comfortable base

Where you stay can shape the entire mood of a short trip. For a quiet weekend, comfort and convenience are often more valuable than having a long list of amenities.

A welcoming room, a pleasant view, a comfortable bed, and easy access to the places you want to explore can make a short stay feel considerably more relaxed.

Leave room for ordinary moments

There is no rule saying every moment of a trip needs to feel extraordinary. Some of the nicest memories can come from ordinary things: finding a bakery in the morning, reading near a window, watching the evening light, or taking a quiet walk after dinner.

These small moments are often exactly what makes a weekend feel different from the usual routine.

Return feeling refreshed

A good weekend getaway does not need to transform your entire life. Sometimes it only needs to give you enough distance to return with a clearer head and a little more energy.

Whether you travel somewhere completely new or choose a familiar place nearby, the goal can be wonderfully simple: slow down, notice where you are, and give yourself permission not to rush.

Keep the weekend simple

The most relaxing escapes are often the ones without too many expectations. Pick somewhere appealing, leave some space in the schedule, and let the destination set the pace.

Sometimes a couple of quiet days are all it takes to make the familiar routine feel a little different when you return.
